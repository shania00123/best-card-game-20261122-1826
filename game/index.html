EMBER COURT

A duel of ember-mages · an original card battler

Outwit the Ashen Rival. Spend embers, summon courtiers, chain one element into the next, and burn the rival's life to zero before yours is spent.

How to Play

  • Goal: reduce the rival to 0 life. You both start at 30.
  • Embers: each turn you gain +1 ember (up to 10) and draw a card.
  • Play a card: click it, or press 1–7. It costs embers (blue gem).
  • Attack: click one of your ready courtiers, then click a rival courtier or the rival portrait. Courtiers rest the turn they are summoned, unless they are Swift.
  • Elements: ▲ Flame beats ◆ Thorn, ◆ Thorn beats ● Tide, ● Tide beats ▲ Flame (+2 attack). ✦ Storm is wild.
  • Resonance: play two cards of the same element in one turn and the second is kindled: +1 attack and +1 health (or +1 damage for edicts).
  • Guard: a guarding courtier must be attacked before the rival can be struck.
  • End turn: click End Turn or press E. Empty deck? You take growing fatigue damage.

Keyboard: 1–7 play cards · E end turn · Esc cancel · M mute
